Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FuelNFix ("FuelNFix", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the FuelNFix Android application and associated web services.

By using FuelNFix, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use FuelNFix, we may process the following information.

1.1  Account Information

If you create or use a FuelNFix account, we may collect and process:

  • Username or email/identifier used for authentication
  • Account credentials in securely processed form
  • User account identifier
  • Authentication and session information
  • Profile information that you voluntarily provide

Passwords are intended to be processed securely and are not stored as readable plaintext passwords.

1.2  Vehicle and Fuel Information

If you use FuelNFix's vehicle-management and fuel-tracking features, information may include:

  • Vehicle details
  • Fuel entries
  • Fuel quantity
  • Fuel cost
  • Fuel price
  • Odometer readings
  • Dates and times
  • Fuel-efficiency calculations
  • Service and maintenance records
  • Oil-change information
  • EV charging-session information
  • Reports and statistics generated from your records

The exact information stored depends on the features you use.

1.3  Cloud-Synchronized Data

FuelNFix may provide cloud storage and synchronization for supported application data.

When cloud functionality is enabled, information you submit through supported features may be transmitted to and stored on FuelNFix's backend infrastructure so that it can be accessed across supported devices or through supported services.

Cloud functionality is not necessarily permanent. As described in Section 8, we reserve the right to discontinue, restrict, modify, or migrate cloud functionality.

1.4  Purchase and Entitlement Information

For Android purchases made through Google Play, FuelNFix's backend may process information required to verify and maintain your entitlement, including:

  • Google Play product identifier
  • Google Play purchase token
  • Google Play order identifier, where available
  • Purchase state
  • Purchase/verification timestamps
  • Purchase acknowledgement state
  • Entitlement and revocation status

The purchase token is used for server-side verification of the purchase with Google Play and for associating the purchased FuelNFix entitlement with your FuelNFix account.

FuelNFix does not receive or store your Google account password.

2

How We Use Information

Information processed by FuelNFix may be used to:

  • Provide account authentication and account-management functionality
  • Store and synchronize your application data
  • Display your vehicles, fuel records, service records, charging records, and reports
  • Calculate fuel-efficiency and other statistics
  • Provide application functionality across supported devices
  • Verify Google Play purchases
  • Grant, maintain, or revoke paid entitlements
  • Prevent unauthorized use of paid functionality
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent purchase-token reuse
  • Maintain application security
  • Diagnose technical problems and service failures
  • Maintain and improve the reliability of the service

We do not use your vehicle or fuel records for purposes unrelated to operating and maintaining FuelNFix unless otherwise disclosed.

3

Android Application Integrity and Security

FuelNFix may use Google Play Integrity and related security mechanisms to verify that authentication requests originate from an authorized Android application environment.

This may involve processing security-related information provided by Google Play services, including application and device integrity verdicts.

These mechanisms are used for security and entitlement protection. They are not intended to identify you personally beyond what is necessary to provide and secure the service.

4

Google Play

Android purchases are processed through Google Play.

FuelNFix may communicate with Google Play's developer services to verify purchases, acknowledge eligible purchases, and determine whether an entitlement remains active.

Google Play independently processes payment information according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies. FuelNFix does not directly process or store your complete payment-card information.

For information about Google's handling of your information, refer to Google's applicable privacy documentation.

5

Data Storage and Security

FuelNFix may use third-party infrastructure providers, cloud infrastructure, databases, authentication services, and application-hosting services to operate its backend.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Security measures may include:

  • Encrypted communications where supported
  • Server-side authentication
  • Signed authentication tokens
  • Access controls
  • Parameterized database queries
  • Purchase-token ownership validation
  • Server-side Google Play purchase verification
  • Server-side entitlement enforcement
  • Application integrity verification
  • Security logging and monitoring

However, no Internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6

Data Retention

We retain account and cloud-stored information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant services, maintain security, comply with applicable obligations, or resolve disputes.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the operational requirements of the service.

If cloud functionality is discontinued, we may retain or delete cloud-stored information according to the applicable service-transition or data-deletion process described in Section 8.

7

Local-Only Data

FuelNFix may support local-only storage and operation.

When using local-only functionality, application data may remain on your device rather than being synchronized to FuelNFix's cloud infrastructure.

Local data is subject to the security of your device, operating system, device backups, and any other services you use with your device.

Uninstalling the application, clearing application data, losing the device, or resetting the device may result in loss of locally stored information if no backup exists.

8

Cloud Service Availability and Possible Discontinuation

FuelNFix's cloud functionality is provided subject to the availability and continued operation of the underlying infrastructure.

Cloud storage and synchronization are not guaranteed to remain available indefinitely.

We may, where reasonably necessary, discontinue, suspend, restrict, modify, migrate, or replace cloud functionality due to circumstances including:

  • Infrastructure availability or failure
  • Hosting or operational constraints
  • Security requirements
  • Maintenance requirements
  • Changes in third-party services
  • Changes in applicable laws or regulations
  • Economic or financial constraints
  • Inability to sustainably operate the cloud infrastructure
  • Changes to the FuelNFix product or service architecture

If cloud services are discontinued, FuelNFix may transition the application toward local-only data storage and operation.

Where reasonably practicable, we may provide users with notice of significant changes and may provide an opportunity to export or otherwise retrieve available cloud-stored information before permanent deletion. However, we cannot guarantee that data export or recovery will always be possible, particularly in cases involving infrastructure failure, security incidents, legal requirements, or circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

Users should maintain their own appropriate backups of important information and should not rely exclusively on FuelNFix's cloud infrastructure as their sole copy of important records.

A change from cloud-supported operation to local-only operation may also affect synchronization and access to previously cloud-stored information.

9

Account Deletion and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your FuelNFix account and associated personal information, subject to information that we may be required or permitted to retain for legitimate operational, security, legal, or financial purposes.

Deleting an account may result in deletion of associated cloud-stored application data.

Local data stored on your device may not automatically be deleted when a server-side account is deleted. You may need to remove local application data separately.

Google Play purchase records may also be subject to Google's own retention requirements and cannot necessarily be deleted by FuelNFix.

10

Data Sharing

FuelNFix does not intend to sell your personal information.

Information may be processed or disclosed where reasonably necessary to:

  • Operate FuelNFix infrastructure
  • Provide authentication and cloud services
  • Verify Google Play purchases
  • Maintain application security
  • Diagnose service problems
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect the rights, property, or safety of FuelNFix, users, or others
  • Respond to lawful requests from authorities

Third-party service providers may process information on behalf of FuelNFix when required to provide infrastructure or other operational services.

11

Children's Privacy

FuelNFix is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to FuelNFix in circumstances where such collection is not permitted, please contact us so that appropriate action can be considered.

12

Cookies and Web Services

The FuelNFix web application may use technically necessary cookies, browser storage, authentication mechanisms, or similar technologies required to maintain sessions and provide functionality.

These mechanisms may be used for authentication, security, preferences, and service operation.

FuelNFix does not use cookies for purposes beyond those necessary or otherwise disclosed.

13

Your Responsibilities

You are responsible for:

  • Maintaining the security of your account credentials
  • Keeping your devices reasonably secure
  • Maintaining backups of important local or cloud data where appropriate
  • Providing accurate account information
  • Not sharing authentication credentials
  • Not attempting to circumvent application or entitlement security mechanisms
